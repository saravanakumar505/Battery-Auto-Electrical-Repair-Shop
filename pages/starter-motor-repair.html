STARTING SYSTEM SPECIALISTS

Starter Motor & Starting Circuit Inspection

Thorough evaluation of starter motors, solenoids, starter relays, ignition trigger circuits, and high-current battery cables for vehicles with no-crank or slow-start issues.

Book Starter Inspection Battery Testing

Important Diagnostic Note: Starter Faults vs. Battery Faults

Symptoms of a failing starter motor frequently overlap with a discharged battery or corroded battery terminals. A clicking sound or slow crank does not automatically mean the starter motor is defective—proper diagnosis tests battery capacity and cable voltage drop first.

01 • SYMPTOMS

Common Starting Symptoms

Single loud click when turning the key, engine failing to crank while lights stay bright, intermittent no-crank when hot, or starter spinning without engaging the flywheel.

02 • CIRCUIT CHECKS

Starting Circuit Inspection

Battery load check, verification of S-terminal trigger voltage from ignition/relay, millivolt drop test across starter cables, and cranking amperage measurement.

03 • SOLUTIONS

Repair or Replacement Evaluation

Starter relay or inhibit switch repair, cleaning or replacement of high-resistance battery cables, and starter solenoid service or complete unit replacement.
